Rei compatibility implementations

The classes in dev.mikoto2000.rei.chatmemory adapt the bounded message-memory
contract of Spring AI 2.1.0 (org.springframework.ai:spring-ai-model and
spring-ai-client-chat). The SQLite store adapts the SQLite behavior of
spring-ai-model-chat-memory-repository-jdbc 2.1.0.
Copyright 2023-present the original author or authors.

GoogleHttpTransportFactory adapts certificate-store and mTLS selection from
google-api-client 2.9.2 GoogleNetHttpTransport and GoogleUtils.
Copyright 2013 Google Inc.

These adaptations retain the existing behavior and data formats while removing
dependencies on retired APIs. Modified files identify the adaptations.
